How to cite

Cite the monograph by its concept DOI, which always resolves to the latest archived version:

Ember Research Lab. Spectral Physics: A Unified Framework. Zenodo.
https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.18946320
@misc{emberlab_spectral,
  author = {Ember Research Lab},
  title  = {Spectral Physics: A Unified Framework},
  year   = {2026},
  doi    = {10.5281/zenodo.18946320},
  publisher = {Zenodo}
}

The archived versions at that DOI may lag the current monograph version.
