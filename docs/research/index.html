Spectral physics

The program asks how much of geometry, quantum mechanics and self-reference can be read off one object: a weighted graph with its Laplacian. Three posits start it: a relational structure with a Laplacian observable (Axiom 1), self-adjoint, positive semi-definite and local dynamics (Axiom 2), and self-referential closure (Axiom 3). They are posits, not results. For any claim about a physical host computed on a simpler stand-in, the claim carries at most the tier of the forgetting step between them; nothing on this page is rated at a higher tier than its ledger row.

Tiers

TagMeaning
T1Proved. In Lean: no sorry, and only mathlib's foundational axioms.
T2Proved conditional on cited, non-vacuous literature results.
T3Conjectural or posited. Nothing at T1 or T2 may rely on it.
T0Trivially true; a placeholder, not evidence.
CLOSED / CONDITIONAL / OPEN / STOPPEDStatus words for a line of work: settled, settled given stated hypotheses, unresolved, abandoned with the reason recorded.
NO / DEGENERATEVerdicts the Lean repository uses for negative results.

"Derives" and "predicts" are used only at T1 or T2. A conditional claim "X given H" is tagged twice: the implication at the tier its proof earns, and X itself at T3 when H is a framework hypothesis.

What the program does not yet reproduce

Any unification claim should open with one question: where is gravity in this picture, and at what tier. Ours: the orientation-free face is CONDITIONAL on cited arguments and framework hypotheses; the orientation-carrying face is OPEN, with only a single clock bit identified. In the formalisation, spacetime and general relativity are placeholder theorems. A construction that reproduced some gauge structure or constants but not gravity would be incomplete however close its numbers; this applies to us. No single group is assumed to contain both the Standard Model and gravity, so an embedding would not count as a pass.

Also not established: a Yang-Mills mass gap in the Clay sense (the ledger shows what is conditional and what is placeholder), the cosmological-constant magnitude, and the values of several parameters that the monograph treats as inputs.
