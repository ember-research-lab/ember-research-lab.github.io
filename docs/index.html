Ember Research Lab

We study what follows when a physical or cognitive system is modelled as a weighted graph whose basic observable is the graph Laplacian, and the system is allowed to model itself. Most of what we have is conditional or open, and every result on this site carries a tier: what is proved, what is conditional on named hypotheses, what is conjecture, and what failed.

Where the program stands on gravity: not reproduced. See the gravity rows in the ledger.

Research lines

Spectral physics
A monograph and a Lean 4 formalisation. Machine-checked core, honest negatives, and a claims ledger.
Alignment (conjectural)
A preprint proposing a two-parameter picture of self-modelling systems. Conjecture throughout; not yet operationalised.

Public tools, chiefly in security.
