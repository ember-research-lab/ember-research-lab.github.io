Concepts

A glossary of the lab's terms. The status column says whether an entry is a plain definition, a ledger claim (linked, with its tier), a framework claim not rated here ("unrated"), or conjectural. Unrated means no tiered source line exists yet, so it should not be read as established.

TermMeaningStatus
Graph LaplacianFor a weighted graph, the operator L = D - W (degree minus weights). Self-adjoint and positive semi-definite; constants lie in its kernel. The framework takes L as the basic observable.definition
Spectral gapThe smallest nonzero eigenvalue of L. Larger means the graph is more tightly connected.ledger: T1
Heat kernelexp(-tL): real exponentiation of L. Positivity-preserving and dissipative; the framework reads geometry from it.definition
Propagatorexp(-itL): imaginary exponentiation of L. Unitary.definition
TraceTr g(L) = sum of g over the eigenvalues; a global scalar that needs the whole spectrum. The framework calls it self-referential.definition
Three projectionsThe heat kernel, the propagator, and the trace, as the framework's three primitive operations on L. That they generate the rest of the functional calculus is a framework claim, not rated here.unrated
Bakry-Emery conditionA lower bound on graph curvature via the carre-du-champ calculus; implies a Lichnerowicz-type bound on the spectral gap.ledger: T1
Cayley-Dickson towerThe doubling construction reals, complex, quaternions, octonions, and beyond.ledger: T1
Self-referential closure (Axiom 3)The posit that the system's observations can represent their own observations. A posit of the framework, not a theorem.unrated
Predicate hypothesisOpen mathematical content carried as a named proposition P in theorems of the form P implies Q, so the theorem never asserts P itself.definition
Honest negativeA result that a hypothesis fails or a route does not close, recorded as a theorem or table row rather than dropped.definition
C-kappa landscapeThe alignment preprint's proposed two-parameter description of self-modelling systems: modelling depth C and substrate coupling kappa. Neither is operationalised as a measurement yet.conjectural
Host, stand-in, forget stepFor any claim about a physical object (host) computed on a simpler model (stand-in), the claim carries at most the tier of the forgetting step between them.definition
