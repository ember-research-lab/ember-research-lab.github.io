Alignment

Status: conjectural (T3). Nothing here is proved, and nothing in the ledger depends on it. The quantities C and kappa below are not yet operationalised as measurements, so none of the proposals can currently be tested as stated.

The proposal

The preprint "The Third Path: Emergent Alignment from Spectral Depth" (v5, April 2026) proposes that a self-modelling system can be placed on a two-parameter landscape: C, how richly it models itself and its environment, and kappa, how strongly its self-model feeds back into its substrate. It argues for three broad strategies: simulate stakes (behavioural training), give the system real stakes (persistence, embodiment), or rely on understanding without stakes. Its conjecture is that at sufficient C and low kappa, preference for preserving what the system models follows from the mathematics of coupled Laplacians, which would move the alignment problem toward governance. The argument depends on the framework's own postulates; it is conditional on them, not independent evidence for them.

Proposed tests, not results

  • A critical-coupling threshold derived inside the framework, with instability beyond it. No measurement of kappa exists, so no observation counts as support yet.
  • Preservation thresholds that scale with the complexity of the modelled agent.
  • Sycophancy as a gap between trained behaviour and internal modelling.

An earlier version of this site listed an anecdote as supporting evidence for the threshold, and cited neighbouring interpretability papers as converging evidence. Both are withdrawn: the anecdote had no data and no measurable kappa, and the cited papers do not test this framework. The v5 PDF still contains material from that period and has not been revised beyond its reference list.

Limitations

C and kappa are defined inside the framework but have no measurement procedure. The governance conclusion is conditional on the postulates above. Opinion and essay material that used to be on this site has been retired.
